Delete your MakeHabits account

Last updated 16 October 2026

In the app

  1. Open MakeHabits and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your friends stop seeing you right away. The account is deleted after 7 days. If you log in before then, you can choose Keep my account.

Without the app

Write from the email address of your account to makehabits.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". We will start the deletion and reply when it is done. It takes at most 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email, username, and timezone.
  • Your habits, check-ins, streaks, and freezes.
  • Your step counts and settings.
  • Your watch photos.
  • Your badges and cheers on them.
  • Your friends, friend requests, shared items, and cheers.
  • Step challenges you started. They also disappear for the other members.
  • A linked Galaxy Watch.

You can also delete a single habit and its history in the app: open the habit and tap Delete.

Crash reports hold only a random id and no email or name. They are removed by Sentry after its own retention period.
