MakeHabits privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

MakeHabits is a habit and step tracker you can use with friends. This page explains what the app stores, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. If anything is unclear, write to makehabits.app@gmail.com.

What the app stores

  • Account: your email address, your username, and your timezone.
  • Habits: the habits you create, the days you mark them done, your streaks and freezes, and reminder times.
  • Steps: your daily step counts, your daily goal, and whether steps come from your phone, your watch, or are added by hand.
  • Friends: friend requests, your friends, what you choose to share with each friend, cheers you send or receive, and step challenges you join.
  • Galaxy Watch link: if you link a watch, a code and a secret that lets that watch send your step count.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a report with the error and a random account id. It does not include your email, username, or habit names.

Why

To show your habits and steps back to you, keep your streaks, and let you share with friends you choose. We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not use it to build profiles.

Steps and Health Connect

On Android, MakeHabits asks permission to read your step count from Health Connect. On iPhone it reads steps from the motion sensor. The app only reads steps. It does not read other health data and does not write anything back to Health Connect. Your steps are used to show your progress, your streak, and your rank among friends who share steps with you.

Who can see your data

  • Only you can see your email.
  • Friends can see your username. They can see a habit or your steps only if you chose to share it with them.
  • Our hosting provider, Supabase, stores the data for us. Sentry receives crash reports. Neither uses your data for its own purposes.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your own phone. They are not sent from a server.

How your data is protected

Data travels between the app and the server over an encrypted connection. Access to each person's data is limited to that person's own account and to what they share.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app: Settings, Delete account. Your friends stop seeing you right away. After 7 days the account and everything in it is deleted for good, including habits, streaks, steps, friends, challenges, and a linked watch. If you log in during those 7 days, you can keep the account. You can also ask for deletion without the app on the delete page.

Children

MakeHabits is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
